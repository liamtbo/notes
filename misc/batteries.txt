mAh
    milliampere-hour — it’s a measure of a battery’s capacity, or how much charge it can store.
    milliampere (mA) = one-thousandth of an ampere (a measure of current)
    hour (h) = time
xS
    battery pack has x cells connected in series.
    S = Series (adds voltage, capacity stays the same)
    Each LiPo (Lithium Polymer) cell is typically 3.7 V nominal (4.2 V fully charged).
    3S = 3 × 3.7 V = 11.1 V nominal (12.6 V fully charged).
c
    C-rate
    tells you how quickly a battery can safely charge or discharge relative to its capacity.
    The C-rate is a multiplier of the battery’s capacity in amp-hours (Ah).
    Current (A)=C×Capacity (Ah)
    1C means you’re charging/discharging the battery in 1 hour.
    2C means in 0.5 hours (twice as fast).
    0.5C means in 2 hours (half as fast).
