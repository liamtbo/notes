Splunk is a data analytics and security company that specializes in processing and analyzing large volumes of machine-generated data. 
Their platform helps organizations monitor, search, analyze, and visualize data in real-time, primarily for cybersecurity, IT operations, and observability.

What Splunk Does:
    Log Management & Analysis – Collects and indexes machine data from applications, servers, IoT devices, and security logs.
    Security Information & Event Management (SIEM) – Detects and responds to cybersecurity threats using real-time analytics.
    IT Operations & Observability – Monitors cloud infrastructure, applications, and networks to prevent downtime and performance issues.
    AI & Machine Learning – Uses ML to detect anomalies, predict system failures, and automate responses.
    Incident Response & SOAR (Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response) – Automates security workflows to respond to threats faster.

Key Products
    Splunk Enterprise – Core platform for analyzing log and machine data.
    Splunk Cloud – Cloud-hosted version of Splunk Enterprise.
    Splunk Observability Cloud – For application and infrastructure monitoring.
    Splunk Security Solutions – SIEM, threat intelligence, and security automation tools.
